# HealthTrack+ 🏥

**HealthTrack+** is a unified, intelligent healthcare ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between patients, medical professionals, and service providers. Our platform leverages advanced AI and real-time data to create a seamless health management experience.

---

## 👁️ Our Vision
To build a **decentralized, AI-first healthcare infrastructure** where quality medical guidance and service accessibility are universal. We envision a world where health data is not just stored, but intelligently used to predict and prevent illnesses before they occur.

---

## 👥 Persona-Based Ecosystem

### 1. 👤 The Client (Patient Portal)
**How it Works:**
The Client portal is the central hub for personal wellness. Users log their daily vitals (BP, sugar, heart rate), track lifestyle habits (water, steps), and monitor mental health.
- **AI-Powered Guidance**: The integrated Nexus AI provides instant answers to medical queries and analyzes logged data for trends.
- **Self-Management**: Patients can schedule medicine reminders, upload digital prescriptions, and manage insurance policies.
- **Direct Access**: Book appointments with doctors for either in-person or video consultations.

**Future Roadmap:**
- **Wearable Sync**: Direct integration with Apple Health, Google Fit, and smartwatches.
- **Predictive Alerts**: AI models that notify users of potential health risks based on long-term data trends.
- **Personalized Wellness**: Gen-AI generated meal and exercise plans tailored to specific health conditions.

---

### 2. 👨‍⚕️ The Doctor (Clinical Portal)
**How it Works:**
The Doctor portal provides a high-level command center for medical professionals to manage their practice and patients.
- **Patient Management**: Secure access to patient health history, records, and reports to make informed decisions.
- **Appointment Lifecycle**: Complete control over scheduling—accepting, rejecting, or marking visits as complete.
- **Telemedicine**: Built-in video consultation tools to reach patients regardless of their location.
- **Clinical Overview**: A dashboard that highlights the "Next Patient" and summarizes daily consultations.

**Future Roadmap:**
- **AI-Assisted Diagnosis**: Preliminary insights from AI to help doctors identify patterns in patient data.
- **Auto-Transcription**: Voice-to-text charting during consultations to reduce administrative burden.
- **Collaborative Care**: Secure channels for multi-doctor case discussions within the platform.

---

### 3. 🏢 The Service Provider (Marketplace Portal)
**How it Works:**
This portal is designed for clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, and even medical transportation services (ambulances/health-cabs).
- **Service Marketplace**: Providers can list their offerings (e.g., Blood Tests, Physiotherapy, Home Care) with custom pricing and duration.
- **Request Management**: A real-time system to accept or decline incoming service requests from patients.
- **Earnings & Analytics**: Track revenue, job completion rates, and user ratings to optimize business performance.
- **Availability Control**: Toggle "Online/Offline" status to manage staff availability dynamically.

**Future Roadmap:**
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Algorithmic pricing models based on demand and availability.
- **Fleet/Staff Management**: Real-time tracking for medical transportation and field staff.
- **Automated Inventory**: Integration with pharmacy systems to track and order medical supplies automatically.

---

### 4. 🔑 The Admin (System Controller)
**How it Works:**
The Admin portal is the specialized command center for platform governance and security.
- **User Governance**: Oversee the verification of doctors and service providers. Admins manually review credentials before granting access to sensitive clinical features.
- **Global Stats**: Real-time tracking of platform-wide metrics, including total users, active health records, and pending support requests.
- **System Integrity**: Monitor encrypted health data flow and manage system-wide reports for performance auditing.
- **Platform Configuration**: Control global settings, maintenance modes, and API security parameters.

**Future Roadmap:**
- **Automated Verification**: Integration with medical license databases for instant doctor verification.
- **Anomaly Detection**: AI-driven monitoring to detect fraudulent activity or security breaches in real-time.
- **Advanced Analytics**: Granular heatmaps of service demand to help guide platform expansion.

## 📂 Tech Stack & Infrastructure

- **Frontend**: Next.js 15, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Recharts.
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL/SQLite.
- **AI Stack**: Llama 3.3, Gemini 2.0, Sarvam AI (Voice).
- **Security**: JWT Auth, Email OTP, AES Encryption for documents.

---

## 🛠️ Getting Started

1. **Backend**: `cd server && npm install && npm run dev`
2. **Frontend**: `cd frontend && npm install && npm run dev`
3. **Environment**: Ensure `.env` files are configured for Groq, OpenRouter, and Firebase.
